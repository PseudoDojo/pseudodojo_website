Legal & Privacy

Here is some information regarding the PseudoDojo project license, website hosting, and privacy.

Privacy Statement

Personal data

The PseudoDojo website does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to access our open-source content. We do not collect, store, or process personal data through this website.

Cookies

PseudoDojo does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or user tracking. The website stores a small number of interface preferences (such as the currently selected pseudo options) locally in your browser using local storage. These preferences remain on your device and are never transmitted to our servers. They may be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Server logs

Like most websites, the web server hosting PseudoDojo may automatically generate standard server logs when pages are requested. These logs may include technical information such as IP address, date and time of access, requested resource, and browser and operating system information.These logs are maintained by the hosting infrastructure for operational, performance, and security purposes and are not used by the PseudoDojo project to identify individual users.
